01From my heart to yours
✳

To my dearest
Ai,

There are a few things
I want you to know.

With love, always.

A letter for the days
you forget how wonderful you are.

Sometimes I wonder how one person can carry so many responsibilities and still find a reason to smile. Then I think of you—not as someone who has to be endlessly strong, but as you: a woman with a lovely, generous heart, making her way through one full day at a time.

I see how hard you work. I know that after an exhausting day, you still show up for your evening classes and try to keep all the little pieces of life moving. I admire your determination. But Ai, you never have to prove your strength to me. You are already worthy of gentleness on the days you have nothing left to give.

You don’t have to smile when you’re hurting. You don’t have to make every hard thing sound manageable. You can bring me the tired version of you, the quiet version, the emotional version, the one who doesn’t know what to do next. I don’t want only the polished moments. I want to understand you through every season, at the pace that feels safe for you.

I can’t go back in time and sit beside you in moments when you needed company. I can only meet you here, in the life we’re living now, and hope to make the days ahead feel a little softer. Not by fixing every difficult thing, but by listening, by caring, by reminding you that you don’t have to earn your place in my heart through how much you do.

I wish I could press pause on the busiest evenings, bring you your favourite food, make us matcha, and sit with you while the world goes quiet. I want to share beautiful, unhurried moments with you—small ones now, and someday, a little trip into the snow you dream about.

One day, I want us to stand beneath the Northern Lights together. Not through a photograph, not through a screen—just the two of us, watching colour move across the sky. And when it happens, I hope you remember this little dream you once let me in on. You deserve to see it come true, Ai. And so much more.

Until then, I hope you keep making room for the things that make you feel alive, too. Your dreams count. Your rest counts. Your happiness counts, even when there’s nothing to show for it at the end of the day.

I’m grateful that our paths met. I’m proud of who you are—on the busy days and the soft, ordinary ones—and I hope you always feel free to be fully yourself with me.

With all my heart,Your favourite person ♡

09The last little note

To my favourite girl,

Ai.

I wrote you one more thing. It can wait until you’re ready.

I don’t know exactly what every chapter of our future will look like. But I know I would love to keep discovering it with you, one page at a time, with room for both of us to grow.

I want to celebrate your little achievements, listen to your stories, share your favourite food, go to places we’ve only dreamed about, and make memories that still make us smile when we’re old.

I want to see you chase the things that light you up. I want to see you happy, peaceful, and proud of yourself—not because you have to be, but because you get to be.

Most of all, I hope you never have to wonder if you deserve to be loved. You do.

Happy Princess Day, my beautiful Aurel. You are precious. You are enough. You are loved.

Forever cheering for you,Your favourite person ♡
